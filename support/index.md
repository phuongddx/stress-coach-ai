# Support

Need help with Stress AI Coach? Email us at [support@stressmonitor.app](mailto:support@stressmonitor.app). Tell us your iPhone and Apple Watch models, their iOS and watchOS versions, and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app).

Stress AI Coach is a wellness app, not a medical device. We can't give medical advice. If you're worried about your health, talk to a healthcare professional.

## Common questions

**My stress score isn't updating.**
The score needs heart rate variability (HRV) from Apple Health, which Apple Watch records a few times a day. See [Measurement frequency](/user-guide/measurement-frequency) and [Manual measurement](/user-guide/manual-measurement).

**Apple Watch notifications or the watch face aren't working.**
See [Watch notification issues](/user-guide/notifications-troubleshoot) and [Watch face not updating](/watch-widget/widget-not-updating).

**Why does my HRV differ from Apple Health?**
See [HRV vs Apple Health](/principle/why-different-from-apple-health).

**What does the AI coach see?**
Ripple sees your derived stress scores, never your raw Health readings. AI replies can be wrong and are not medical advice. The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) has the details.

More answers are in the [FAQ](/principle/frequently-asked-questions) and on the [home page](https://phuongddx.github.io/stress-coach-ai/#faq).

## Subscriptions

Stress AI Coach Plus is billed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open the iPhone **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**. For a refund, use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, use **Restore purchases** on the Plus screen in the app.

## Delete your account and data

- **Account:** in the app, open **Settings → Account → Delete Account** and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and the data stored with it on our server. It cannot be undone.
- **Data on your iPhone:** open **Settings → Data & support → Manage data** to delete stress snapshots stored on the device, by date range or all at once.
- **Apple Health:** the app only reads from Apple Health and never writes to it. To stop access, open the iPhone **Settings** app, then **Privacy & Security → Health → Stress AI Coach**.

If you can't open the app, you can request deletion of your personal information by emailing [support@stressmonitor.app](mailto:support@stressmonitor.app), as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).
