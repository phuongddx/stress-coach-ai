# Delete your Stress AI Coach account

This page explains how to delete your Stress AI Coach account and the data stored with it, from inside the app or without the app. Questions: [support@stressmonitor.app](mailto:support@stressmonitor.app).

## Delete in the app

1. Open Stress AI Coach.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**.
3. Confirm. The app warns you first: this permanently deletes your account and its data, and it cannot be undone.

## Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app or can't open it, email [support@stressmonitor.app](mailto:support@stressmonitor.app) with the subject **Delete my account**. Include:

- how you signed in (Google or anonymous), and
- for Google sign-in, the Google account email you used.

We use this only to find and delete your account, and we reply to confirm when it is done. An anonymous account has no email or name attached, so we may not be able to find it from an email alone. If you signed in anonymously and still have the app, deleting in the app is the reliable way.

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove from our server:

- your chat sessions and messages with the coach, including the stress context sent with them,
- your preferences and consent records,
- your credit balance, credit history and subscription status, and
- your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).

## What is kept, and for how long

- **An opaque account ID.** We keep the random account identifier, with no other data attached, so a deleted account can't be recreated by mistake and so we can finish removing the sign-in account. It is erased 30 days after the sign-in account is removed.
- **Purchase records held by the store.** Apple keeps its own records of purchases and subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your store account (on iPhone: **Settings → your name → Subscriptions**).

## Data on your devices

- **Stress data on your iPhone:** stress snapshots are stored on your device, not on our server. Delete them in the app under **Settings → Data & support → Manage data**, or delete the app.
- **Apple Health:** the app only reads from Apple Health and never writes to it. Health data never reaches our server. To stop access, open the iPhone **Settings** app, then **Privacy & Security → Health → Stress AI Coach**.

See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for more on how data is handled.
