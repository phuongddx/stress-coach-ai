Email support

Do I need an Apple Watch?

It works best with one. The app needs iOS 18.6 or later, and Apple Health is optional but recommended: your watch records the HRV and heart rate the score reads.

What does the AI coach see?

Only derived scores, such as your stress score and its factors. Your raw health data stays on your iPhone, and the optional health sync asks for your consent first.

How does the 14-day trial work?

The yearly Plus plan starts with 14 days free. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, it renews at $25.99 a year.

How do I cancel the Plus subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal and you are not charged again.
