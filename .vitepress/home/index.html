How the score works

One stress score from five Apple Health signals

The score blends five signals from Apple Health into one reading from 0 to 100, so you never have to read five charts to know how your day is going.

  1. 1

    Computed on your iPhone

    From the readings your Apple Watch and Apple Health already collect.

  2. 2

    Honest about confidence

    Every reading shows how confident it is, so you know how much weight to give it.

  3. 3

    Names what moved it

    Sleep, activity or something else: each reading points to the factor behind it.

  • Relaxed0–25
  • Mild25–50
  • Elevated50–75
  • High75–90
  • Severe90–100

Guided breathing

Box breathing: two minutes, four sides

Box breathing is the reset Ripple suggests most: in, hold, out, hold, four seconds each. Try one round here, the same rhythm the app uses.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

It works best with one: the score is built from the heart rate variability your watch records, along with heart rate, sleep, activity and recovery from Apple Health.

Apple Health is optional but recommended. The app needs iOS 18.6 or later.

What does the AI coach see?

Only derived scores: your stress score and its factors. Raw health data stays on your iPhone, and optional health sync asks for your consent first.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly Plus plan ($25.99 a year) starts with a 14-day free trial. The monthly plan ($4.99 a month) has no trial. Plus is billed to your Apple ID and renews until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions and cancel Stress AI Coach at least 24 hours before it renews. You can cancel any time.
