How the score works

Five signals in. One calm number out.

The stress score blends five signals from Apple Health into a single reading from 0 to 100, so you do not have to read five charts to know how your day is going.

  1. HRV
  2. Heart rate
  3. Sleep
  4. Activity
  5. Recovery
0–100Your stress score

All five signals come from Apple Health.

Five levels, one for every reading.

Each score lands on one of five levels, and Ripple’s face changes with it.

  • Relaxed0–25
  • Mild25–50
  • Elevated50–75
  • High75–90
  • Severe90–100

Computed on your iPhone

The score is calculated on the iPhone itself, from the readings your Apple Watch and Apple Health already collect.

Honest about confidence

Every reading shows how confident it is, so you know how much weight to give it.

Names what moved it

Each reading points to the factor that moved it, so you can see whether it was sleep, activity or something else.

Privacy

Your health data stays home.

The short version, in four points.

  • Raw health data stays on your iPhone.

    Your readings from Apple Health are turned into a score on the iPhone itself.

  • Your history, in your own iCloud.

    Optional iCloud sync keeps your stress history in your own iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted by Apple. We cannot access it.

  • No ads. No selling.

    No ads, no advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.

  • Ripple sees scores, not raw data.

    Ripple only sees derived scores (your stress score and its factors), never raw health data.

FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else: support@stressmonitor.app

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Stress AI Coach works best with Apple Watch: the score is built from the heart rate variability your watch records, along with heart rate, sleep, activity and recovery from Apple Health.

Apple Health is optional but recommended. The app requires iOS 18.6 or later.

What does the AI coach see?

Only derived scores: your stress score and its factors. Raw health data stays on your iPhone, and optional health sync asks for your consent first.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly Plus plan ($25.99 / year) starts with a 14-day free trial. The monthly plan ($4.99 / month) has no trial.

Plus is billed to your Apple ID and renews until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions and cancel Stress AI Coach at least 24 hours before it renews. You can cancel any time.
